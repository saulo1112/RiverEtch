# Magdalena regulatory verification

**Status:** Step 2 of the Magdalena validation, read on 2026-10-08, before the full run. Every quote
below was copied from the text that `climate-pipeline fetch-docs` extracted from the fetched file,
and re-checked against that text and page. The fetch record (status, final URL, bytes, SHA-256, UTC
time of every source) is [sources/fetch-manifest.csv](sources/fetch-manifest.csv); the source list
is [sources/regulatory-sources.csv](sources/regulatory-sources.csv). No document is committed.

> This file records what public documents say. It makes no claim that any actor caused anything,
> and none of it is an accusation. Spanish appears only inside quotes, each followed by an English
> translation (labelled "tr.").

Type: primary = the issuing authority's, the contracting entity's or the open-data publisher's own
record; secondary = a document reporting another. Confidence: high = primary, read on the page;
medium = secondary, or primary but indirect; low = a single opinion source. All sources fetched
2026-10-08 (UTC); ids refer to the manifest. Pages are PDF pages.

## Verdict

Defined in [method.md](method.md#question-3-regulation-and-public-data) before this verification:

| Verdict | Meaning                                                                               | What the demo may say                                                                                                           |
| ------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| (a)     | A real condition sets a limit the observation can be compared with                    | Cite that condition with its source; still no accusation                                                                        |
| (b)     | The authority and the instrument are verified, but no condition is a comparable limit | Permit holder "simulated permit holder"; limit "illustrative limit, not the real licence"; real names only in the context block |
| (c)     | The authority or the instrument cannot be verified from a primary source              | Same labels as (b), and no entity names in the interface                                                                        |

**Evidence points to (c).** A person decides; this file does not. The reasons:

1. **The current contract is verified; its instrument is not.** The open contract record shows
   maintenance dredging of the navigation channel between Barrancabermeja and Barranquilla, a
   stretch that contains the reach. The contract runs from 2024-01-04 to 2026-12-31. Its tender
   documents (technical annex, environmental obligations) sit behind a reCAPTCHA on SECOP II and
   could not be read.
2. **No licensing authority applies on the texts read.** Decree 1076 of 2015 makes only
   "dragados de profundización" (deepening dredging) subject to an environmental licence. The 2021
   tender for the same sector states that maintenance dredging "no requiere de Licencia Ambiental"
   (tr. "does not require an Environmental Licence"). It puts environmental management under a
   PAGA (a programme adapting the environmental guide), written by the contractor and approved by
   the supervising contractor. Local environmental permits are left to the contractor "según su
   aplicación" (tr. "as applicable").
   - Whether the regional authorities (CAS for Santander, CSB for southern Bolívar) issued any
     permit for this reach was not found.
   - Cormagdalena's 2013 environmental management plan for maintenance dredging was not found.
3. **No numeric limit was found.** No document read sets a numeric turbidity or suspended-solids
   limit for this dredging.
   - The 2011 environmental guide _recommends_ monitoring turbidity and suspended solids 50 m
     upstream and downstream of works. It requires the dredge plume to stay within the approved
     area.
   - The 2021 tender asks for water and sediment quality results in monthly reports, without
     parameters or limits.

**Recommendation:** keep the interface as configured: "simulated permit holder", "illustrative
limit, not the real licence", no entity names. Real names stay in `context.sources` only. If a person
obtains the 2024-2026 contract's annex and its PAGA, the verdict can be revisited. Even then, a
numeric limit is unlikely on the evidence above.

## 7. Sensitivity scan

Neutral, sourced, one line each. None of these is an observation of the satellite series.

- **Fracking research pilots in Puerto Wilches.** ANLA licensed the Kalé pilot (Resolution 00648
  of 25 February 2022, per [Vanguardia, 2022-03-28](https://www.vanguardia.com/economia/nacional/2022/03/28/anla-aprobo-licencia-ambiental-para-piloto-de-fracking-en-puerto-wilches-santander/),
  `vanguardia-kale-licencia-2022`). In November 2022 the company was reported to be halting the
  Kalé and Platero pilots, after a September request to the hydrocarbons agency to suspend them
  ([Portafolio, 2022-11-09](https://www.portafolio.co/negocios/empresas/ecopetrol-ya-no-hara-fracking-en-colombia-sus-razones-573820),
  `portafolio-fracking-2022`). The pilots are on land; the topic is locally contested.
- **Floods and river-bank dikes.** High water was reported overflowing at Sitio Nuevo and Vijagual
  (north of Puerto Wilches) in May 2024 (`vanguardia-sos-2024`), threatening dikes in March 2025
  (`vanguardia-riesgo-2025`), and under a red alert in May 2025 (`vanguardia-alerta-2025`). A dike
  breach at Sitio Nuevo was being closed with a lent dredge until May 2023
  (`vanguardia-sitio-nuevo-2023`). Sitio Nuevo lies downstream of the reach.
- **Dredging disposal and Cantagallo (single opinion source).** An opinion column states that
  dredging works off Puerto Wilches in 2014-2015 deposited sediment into the arm that passed
  Cantagallo and closed its river access ([Las2orillas, 2024-10-25](https://www.las2orillas.co/el-rio-magdalena-y-su-lenta-muerte-frente-a-todos/),
  `las2orillas-cantagallo`). This is the author's claim. It was not checked against any other
  source, and it predates the study period.
- **Navigation recovery project.** Past criticism of the channel-recovery project for proceeding
  without an environmental licence is reported by El Tiempo (2017-04-27, `eltiempo-navelena`).
