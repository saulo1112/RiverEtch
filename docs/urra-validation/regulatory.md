## Verdict

**Verdict (b): the licensee is verified from primary sources, but no licence condition is a limit
the observation can be compared with.**

- **Licensee:** URRÁ S.A. E.S.P. (tax id NIT 800175746-9) holds the environmental licence of the
  Urrá I hydroelectric project (ANLA case file LAM0112). The company says its plant is run under
  contract by Emec S.A.S.
- **Licence conditions:** the licence sets minimum dissolved oxygen, a monthly band of discharges,
  sediment survey campaigns and weekly turbidity _monitoring_ below the dam. It sets no turbidity
  limit. ANLA itself wrote that no specific turbidity value is set (quote C6). The only numeric
  target on suspended material is a monitoring-plan indicator in milligrams per litre of suspended
  solids (ICOSUS < 1, quote C9), measured by the operator at its own stations. A Sentinel-2 NDTI is
  a relative index that cannot be converted to NTU or mg/L without co-located field data, and none
  is public for this reach.
- **Recommendation for the demo:** keep the real reach and its real Sentinel-2 series. Any permit
  in the demo is labelled as simulated: holder **"simulated permit holder"**, limit
  **"illustrative limit, not the real licence"**. The user interface names the river and the
  municipality, never the dam or the company. Real names appear only in a separate context block
  marked "public-source context, not an accusation". This matters more here because ANLA
  announced a sanctioning procedure against the licensee in February 2026 (see
  [Sensitivity scan](#sensitivity-scan)).

## Permit versus observation

**No.** The observation is a relative turbidity index (NDTI) on two 2 km sub-reaches roughly 2 to
15 km below the dam's outlet (approximate). It is comparable only with its own baseline.

- No condition sets a turbidity limit (C6).
- The suspended-solids target is in mg/L, on the operator's samples (C9). NDTI cannot be expressed
  in mg/L or NTU without co-located field data, and none is public for the reach.
- Discharge and oxygen conditions (C3-C5) are not observable by Sentinel-2 NDTI.
- At most, the licence and the observation share a concern: the licence makes turbidity and
  suspended solids measured variables just below the dam. A proof could honestly attest "the NDTI
  of this reach departed from its own baseline", never "a licence limit was exceeded".

## Sensitivity scan

Public facts that make any statement about this reach sensitive. Each is reported as the source
states it, without judgment.

- **An open sanctioning procedure.** ANLA's news item of 9 February 2026
  (`anla-news-sobrepaso-2026`): "La Autoridad Nacional de Licencias Ambientales ha ordenado un
  procedimiento sancionatorio a la empresa operante, URRÁ S.A. E.S.P., por la presunta superación
  sistemática del volumen máximo del embalse." ("ANLA has ordered a sanctioning procedure against
  the operating company, URRÁ S.A. E.S.P., for the alleged systematic exceedance of the reservoir's
  maximum volume.") The item refers to 2020-2026, overlapping the study period. It concerns
  reservoir volume, which NDTI does not measure. A demo anomaly shown next to the company's name
  could be read as taking a side in that procedure.
- **An authority finding of February 2026.** Auto 000939/2026, p. 20, after reviewing the
  discharges of January and February 2026: "configurándose el incumplimiento de la medida asociada
  a las reglas de operación del embalse, con potencial afectación al régimen hidrológico del río
  Sinú aguas abajo, independientemente de que no se hayan evidenciado afectaciones físicas en la
  infraestructura del proyecto." ("constituting non-compliance with the measure on the reservoir's
  operating rules, with potential effects on the hydrological regime of the Sinú downstream,
  regardless of whether physical damage to the project's infrastructure was observed.") This is
  after the study period (it ends 31 Dec 2025).
- **Floods and displaced families in February 2026**, described in the same news item and auto.
- **Indigenous rights litigation.** Constitutional Court ruling T-652 of 1998 (`cc-t-652-98`)
  ordered: "Tercero. ORDENAR a la Empresa Multipropósito Urrá s.a. que indemnice al pueblo
  Embera-Katío del Alto Sinú" ("Third. ORDER the company Empresa Multipropósito Urrá S.A. to
  compensate the Embera-Katío people of the Alto Sinú"). Ruling T-194 of 1999 (`cc-t-194-99`)
